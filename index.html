Less Memorizing, More Understanding

I have decided to build a website based on education.

The Problem

I have noticed that most students pass exams but dont truly understand. Worldwide, memorization replaces real learning.

Idea

That is why I have decided to build a website that will deliver personalized, fun and impactful learning by using Montessori-inspired methods: hands-on tools, child centered lessons, and learning by discovery so students don't memorize - they understand.

Who It Helps

Students:Learn in a way that suits you.

Teachers:Get lesson ideas and brain storming tools yo make class interesting.

How Someone Would Try It Out Today

Just visit the website (it would be called Studify). Tell me if you are a student or a teacher. Take a small test and your best method of learning would be recommended to you. With that, you would enjoy the fun journey of learning laid ahead of you.

My Next Steps

  1. Build the website.
  2. Ask teachers, students, and mentors what they think so i can make it better.
  3. Make it known through social media so more students and teachers can use it.
